# Your First Order — Complete Walkthrough

This guide walks you through creating a new stitching order from start to finish.

## Step 1 — Open the Order List and Tap +

Go to the **Orders** tab at the bottom of the screen. Tap the **+** button in the top-right corner. A menu appears with three order types:

- **New Stitching** — for tailoring/stitching jobs
- **New Material Sale** — for selling cloth/fabric
- **New Readymade Sale** — for selling readymade garments

Tap **New Stitching** to create a stitching order.

![Order list with + menu](/img/screenshots/photo_14_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 2 — Select or Add a Customer

The **Select Customer** screen opens. If you have existing customers, search and tap one to select them.

To add a new customer, tap **+** in the top-right corner and choose:
- **Add New Contact** — enter details manually
- **Select a Phone Contact** — import from your phone's contacts

![Select customer screen](/img/screenshots/photo_15_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

![Add new contact options](/img/screenshots/photo_16_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

### Adding a New Customer

Fill in the customer details:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Required |
| **Phone Number** | With country code |
| **Email** | Optional |
| **Date of Birth** | Optional |
| **Gender** | Male / Female |
| **Height / Weight** | Optional |
| **How did they find you?** | Optional |
| **Photos** | Face and body photos (optional) |

Tap **Save & Continue**.

![Enter customer details](/img/screenshots/photo_17_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 3 — Select the Garment Item

The **Select Item** screen shows suggested items and the full list. Tap the garment type you are stitching (e.g. Kurti, Churidar, Salwar Kameez).

![Select item screen](/img/screenshots/photo_18_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 4 — Enter Stitch Item Details

The **New Stitch Item** screen opens for the selected garment. Here you can:

- Choose **Stitching** or **Alteration**
- Tap **Add Measurement** to enter the customer's measurements
- Tap **Select Sleeve, Front Neck etc.** to choose stitching style options
- Add **Special Instructions** as text or record an audio note
- Add a **Video Link** for reference
- Toggle **Measurement dress given** if the customer has provided a reference garment
- Set the **Number of Dress Items**

![New stitch item details](/img/screenshots/photo_19_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

### Adding Measurements

Tap **Add Measurement** to open the measurements form. Enter all relevant measurements in inches (Length, Shoulder, Upper Chest, Bust, Waist, Sleeve Length, etc.). Tap **Save Measurements** when done.

![Enter measurements](/img/screenshots/photo_20_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

![Measurements continued](/img/screenshots/photo_21_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

### Selecting Stitching Options

Tap **Select Sleeve, Front Neck etc.** to pick style options such as Sleeve Type (Half, Full, No, Cap) and Front Neck Design (U-Neck, V-Neck, Round, etc.). Tap **Save Options**.

![Stitching style options](/img/screenshots/photo_22_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

### Setting the Price

Scroll down to **Stitching Charge**. Enter the base price and optionally add line-item add-ons (e.g. Lace, Embroidery) using the **Add Price Breakup** popup. The total is calculated automatically. Tap **Confirm Price**.

![Price breakup](/img/screenshots/photo_23_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

### Adding Cloth Images

Tap the cloth image slots to photograph or attach the customer's cloth. If there are multiple dress items, assign each cloth photo to the correct item number.

![Cloth image — item 1](/img/screenshots/photo_24_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

![Cloth image — item 2](/img/screenshots/photo_25_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

### Setting Dates

- **Delivery Date** — when the order will be ready for pickup
- **Trial Date** *(optional)* — for a fitting session before final delivery
- **Function Date** *(optional)* — if the garment is needed for a specific event
- **Mark as Urgent** — toggle on if this order needs priority

You can also assign a **Sales Staff** member to the order.

![Dates and delivery settings](/img/screenshots/photo_26_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 5 — Order Created

Once saved, the order summary screen shows the order number (e.g. **Order#XD489**), each stitch item with its price and delivery date, and the payment summary at the bottom.

![Order summary screen](/img/screenshots/photo_27_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 6 — Record a Payment (Optional)

To collect an advance or full payment, tap **Receive Payment**. Enter:

- **Payment Date**
- **Amount**
- **Payment Method** (Cash, UPI, Card, etc.)

Tap **Save Payment**.

![Receive payment](/img/screenshots/photo_28_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

The order summary updates to show the **Payments Received** and remaining **Balance Due**. From here you can also:

- Tap **Receive Payment** again to record additional payments
- Expand **Discount** to apply a discount amount and tap **Apply**

![Order after payment with Finish Order button](/img/screenshots/photo_29_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

![Applying a discount](/img/screenshots/photo_30_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

![Order summary with discount applied](/img/screenshots/photo_31_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 7 — Finish the Order

Once you are done entering all details, tap **Finish Order**. You can also tap **View Bill** to preview the bill before finishing.

The order is now saved and appears in your **Order List** under the **Active** tab with the customer name, order number, status, total amount, item count, and due date.

![Order appears in Order List](/img/screenshots/photo_32_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

---

:::tip
Returning customers already have their measurements saved — so you can skip straight to Step 3. Just select the customer and their previous measurements will be available to reuse.
:::
