# Install TailorMate App

TailorMate is available on Android and iOS, as well as a web browser. Choose the platform that suits you.

## Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android device.
2. Search for **TailorMate**.
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to complete.
4. Tap **Open** to launch the app.

<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tailormate&hl=en_IN" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
  <img src="/img/google-play-badge.png" alt="Get it on Google Play" style={{height: '80px', width: 'auto', marginTop: '0.5rem'}} />
</a>

## iPhone / iPad (iOS)

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Search for **TailorMate**.
3. Tap **Get** and authenticate with Face ID or your Apple ID password.
4. Tap **Open** once the installation is complete.

<a href="https://apps.apple.com/in/app/tailormate-app-for-tailors/id6736565286" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
  <img src="/img/app-store-badge.png" alt="Download on the App Store" style={{height: '80px', width: 'auto', marginTop: '0.5rem'}} />
</a>

## Web Browser

No installation needed. Visit [web.tailormateapp.com](https://web.tailormateapp.com/auth/login) from any browser on your computer or tablet.

---

:::tip
The mobile app and web app stay in sync — any changes you make on one device appear on all others automatically.
:::
