# Create Your Account

Creating a TailorMate account takes about two minutes. You'll set up your login credentials and your shop profile in one go.

## Welcome Screen

When you open TailorMate for the first time, you'll see the welcome screen. Tap **Let's Get Started** to begin the sign-up process. If you already have an account, tap **Log In** instead.

![Welcome screen](/img/screenshots/welcome-screen.jpg)

## Step 1 — Sign Up

1. Open the TailorMate app and tap **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your **Name** and **Email Address**.
3. Tap **Signup**.

![Sign up screen](/img/screenshots/photo_2_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 2 — Verify Your Phone Number

1. Enter your **mobile number** (with country code).
2. Tap **Next**.
![Phone number and OTP verification](/img/screenshots/photo_4_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

3. Check your WhatsApp — TailorMate sends the OTP via **WhatsApp**. If you dont have WhatsApp, you can get OTP from your **email**.
4. Enter the 4-digit OTP and tap **Verify**.

![Phone number and OTP verification](/img/screenshots/photo_6_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 3 — Set Up Your Shop (Part 1)

After verifying your number, you'll be guided through a two-step shop setup.

Fill in your shop details:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Shop Name** | The name that appears on bills |
| **Shop Code** | A short unique identifier for your shop |
| **Shop Type** | Outlet, Workshop, or Both |
| **Stitching For** | Gents, Ladies, or Both |
| **Phone Number** | Shop contact number |
| **UPI ID** | For payment QR code on bills (optional) |
| **Email** | Shop email (optional) |
| **Website / Instagram / WhatsApp / Facebook** | Social/contact links (optional) |

Tap **Next** to continue.

![Shop setup step 1](/img/screenshots/photo_8_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Step 4 — Set Up Your Shop (Part 2)

1. Enter your **Shop Address**.
2. Tap the logo placeholder to upload your **Shop Logo** (from gallery or camera).
3. Add **Shop Photos** if you'd like (optional).
4. Tap **Finish Setup**.

![Shop setup step 2](/img/screenshots/photo_11_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

Your account is now ready and you'll land on the main dashboard.

## Log In

TailorMate uses your **mobile number** and a **WhatsApp OTP** to log you in — no password needed.

### Step 1 — Enter Your Phone Number

1. Open TailorMate and tap **Log In**.
2. Enter your mobile number with the country code.
3. Tap **Next**.

![Enter phone number](/img/screenshots/login-1.jpg)

### Step 2 — Enter the OTP

TailorMate sends a 4-digit verification code to your **WhatsApp** (and email). Open WhatsApp, copy the code, and enter it in the app.

![Enter OTP](/img/screenshots/login-2.jpg)

![OTP received on WhatsApp](/img/screenshots/login-otp.png)

:::tip
If you don't receive the code, tap **I haven't received a code** to resend it.
:::

---

:::tip
You can update your shop profile anytime from **Settings**.
:::
