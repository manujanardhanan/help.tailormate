# Contact Support

Our support team is here to help. Choose the method that works best for you.

## Website Contact Form

The fastest way to reach us:

[Contact Us — tailormateapp.com](https://www.tailormateapp.com/#contact)

Fill in your name, email, and message — we'll get back to you within 1 business day.

---

## Email

Send us an email with your question or issue and we'll respond promptly.

> **support@tailormateapp.com**

Include:
- Your **registered email address**
- A short **description of the issue**
- **Screenshots** if relevant

---

## In-App Feedback

1. Open TailorMate and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Send Feedback**.
3. Describe your issue or suggestion and tap **Submit**.

---

## App Store Reviews

Found a bug or have a feature request? You can also leave feedback through the app stores:

- [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tailormate&hl=en_IN)
