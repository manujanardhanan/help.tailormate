# Reports and Analytics

The Reports section gives you a clear picture of how your business is performing — revenue, orders, and outstanding payments.

## How to Open Reports

Go to **Reports** from the main navigation (bottom bar on mobile, left sidebar on web).

## What Reports Are Available

### Revenue Summary
Shows your total earnings for a selected time period.

- **Total billed** — Sum of all order prices in the period
- **Total received** — Payments actually collected
- **Outstanding** — Amount still owed by customers

### Order Summary
A breakdown of orders by status:
- How many orders are Pending, In Progress, Ready, Delivered, or Cancelled

### Customer Report
Shows which customers have placed the most orders and how much they've spent.

## Filter by Date Range

1. Open **Reports**.
2. Tap the **date filter** at the top.
3. Select a preset (**Today**, **This Week**, **This Month**) or set a **custom range**.
4. The report updates automatically.

## Export or Share a Report

1. Open the report you want to share.
2. Tap the **Share** icon (top right).
3. Choose to share as PDF or screenshot via WhatsApp, email, or save to your device.

---

:::tip
Check the Revenue Summary at the end of each month to see how much is still outstanding, and follow up with customers who haven't paid in full.
:::
