# Managing Customers

The Customers section is where you store contact details and measurement profiles for everyone you work with.

## Add a New Customer

1. Go to **Customers** from the hamburger menu (☰).
2. Tap the **+** button.
3. Fill in the customer details:
   - **Name** and **Phone Number** (required)
   - **Email Address** (optional)
   - **Gender**
   - **Height** and **Weight** (optional — useful for measurement reference)
   - **Profile Photo** (optional)
4. Tap **Save**.

## Edit a Customer

1. Open **Customers** and tap on the customer's name.
2. Tap the **Edit** (pencil) icon.
3. Update the details and tap **Save**.

## View Customer History

Tap any customer to open their profile. You'll see:
- Their saved **measurements**
- All **past and current orders**
- **Payment history**

## Search for a Customer

Use the **search bar** at the top of the Customers screen to find a customer by name or phone number.

## Delete a Customer

1. Open the customer's profile.
2. Tap **More options** (three dots) → **Delete**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

:::caution
Deleting a customer removes their profile and all associated orders permanently. This cannot be undone.
:::
