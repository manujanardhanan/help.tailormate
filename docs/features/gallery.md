# Gallery

The Gallery stores design photos organised by garment type. Use it to save reference photos and show customers what you've made.

Tap **Gallery** in the bottom navigation bar.

## How the Gallery is Organised

Photos are grouped into **folders by garment type**. The default folders match the garment types in your shop (e.g., Churidar, Dress, Frock, Jumpsuit, Kurti, Palazzo, Saree Blouse).

![Gallery folders](/img/screenshots/photo_70_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Add a New Folder

1. Tap **Add New Folder** from the Gallery screen.
2. Enter a folder name.
3. Tap **Save**.

## Add Photos to a Folder

1. Open the folder.
2. Tap **+** or **Add Photo**.
3. Choose a photo from your gallery or take a new one with the camera.

## Browse and Share

Open any folder to browse the photos inside. Tap a photo to view it full-size. Use the share button on a photo to send it via WhatsApp or any other app.

---

:::tip
Keep the Gallery up to date with your best work — it's easy to show customers design options directly from the app during a measurement appointment.
:::
