# Billing and Payments

TailorMate tracks every payment against an order so you always know what's been paid and what's outstanding.

## Record a Payment

1. Open the order from the **Orders** list.
2. Tap **Receive Payment**.
3. Fill in the payment details:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Date the payment was received |
| **Amount** | Amount received in this transaction |
| **Payment Method** | Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque, or Other |
| **Discount** | Apply a discount amount (deducted from the balance) |

4. Tap **Save**.

![Record payment screen](/img/screenshots/photo_27_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Partial Payments (Advance)

Record each payment separately as you receive it. TailorMate automatically tracks the running balance:

**Outstanding Balance = Order Price − Total Payments Received**

You can record an advance at booking, another payment at trial, and the final amount at delivery — all tracked separately.

## View Payment History

Open any order to see the full list of payments recorded, the total paid so far, and the remaining balance.

## Generate a Bill

1. Open the order.
2. Tap **Generate Bill** (or **View Bill**).
3. Choose the bill format:
   - **Standard** — a clean bill layout for sharing with customers
   - **GST TAX Invoice A4** — full GST-compliant invoice on A4 size

![Bill format selection](/img/screenshots/photo_32_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## Print a Bill

From the bill view, tap **Print** and choose your printer method:

| Method | Details |
|---|---|
| **WiFi / PDF** | Print via a WiFi printer or save as PDF to your device |
| **Thermal Bluetooth** | Connect a Bluetooth thermal printer |

For thermal printing, select the paper width: **58mm**, **80mm**, or **100mm**.

## Share a Bill

Tap **Share** on the bill to send it to the customer:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy Link** | Copies a link to the digital bill |
| **Share Bill** | Opens the standard share sheet (any app) |
| **Send Bill** | Sends via TailorMate's built-in messaging |
| **WhatsApp Bill** | Opens WhatsApp with the bill pre-attached |

![Bill share options](/img/screenshots/photo_36_2026-04-26_10-46-17.jpg)

## What a Bill Includes

- Your shop name, address, phone number, and logo
- UPI QR code (if configured in your shop profile)
- Customer name and phone number
- Garment type and order details
- Add-ons (embroidery, lace, etc.) with individual prices
- Order price, amount paid, and balance remaining
- Date of bill generation
- Bill terms (if configured in Administration)

---

:::tip
You can generate and share a bill at any stage — useful for sending an advance receipt right at booking.
:::
