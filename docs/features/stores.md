# Stores

The Stores section shows all the shop locations linked to your TailorMate account and lets you manage your subscription plan.

Go to **☰** → **Stores**.

## Your Store Listing

Each store shows:
- **Shop name and location**
- **Store type** (Outlet, Workshop, or Both)
- **Current plan** (Free Trial or active subscription)
- **Expiry date** of the current plan
- **Renew** button to extend your subscription

## Add a New Store

Tap the **+** button at the top right to add another shop location. Each store has its own orders, customers, and settings.

## Switch Between Stores

If you have multiple stores, tap on a store to switch to it. All data shown in the app (orders, customers, reports) will be for the selected store.

## Subscription Plan

Your plan and expiry date are shown on each store card. Tap **Renew** to extend your subscription before it expires.

:::tip
A **Free Trial** plan gives you access to all features for a limited period. Renew before the expiry date to avoid any interruption to your shop's operations.
:::
