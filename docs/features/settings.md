# App Settings

Settings lets you configure how TailorMate works for your shop — from the services you offer to how bills are numbered.

## How to Open Settings

Tap the **hamburger menu** (☰) and select **Settings**.

## Services Offered

Toggle which services your shop provides:

- **Stitching** — standard tailoring
- **Pickup at Customer Site** — you collect cloth from the customer's location
- **Measurement at Customer Site** — you visit the customer for measurements
- **Online Orders** — accept orders placed online

## Measurement Unit

Choose whether measurements are recorded in **Centimetres (Cms)** or **Inches**. This setting applies across all measurement fields in the app.

## GST Bill

Enable this to generate **GST-compliant tax invoices**. When turned on, you can select the GST TAX Invoice A4 format when generating bills.

## Order Number Format

Control how order numbers are assigned:

| Format | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Random** | Each order gets a random unique number |
| **Serial** | Orders are numbered 1, 2, 3 in sequence |
| **Custom** | You define the prefix and starting number |

## App Customisation

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Message Timing** | When delivery/payment reminder messages are sent |
| **Bill Terms** | Default terms and conditions printed on bills |
| **Bill Number** | Starting number for bill numbering |
| **Hide Standard Dress Items** | Hides the default garment list so only your custom items show |
| **Days to Show Orders** | How many days' worth of orders are visible on the Orders screen |

## Templates

Configure the layout and content of your invoices and PDF bills. Customise what appears in the header, footer, and body of each bill format.

## Change Password

1. Go to **Settings** → **Account**.
2. Tap **Change Password**.
3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
4. Tap **Update**.

## Delete My Account

Found at the bottom of Settings. This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

:::caution
Deleting your account cannot be undone. All your shop data, customers, orders, and measurements will be permanently deleted.
:::

## Log Out

1. Scroll to the bottom of **Settings**.
2. Tap **Log Out**.
3. Confirm.

:::tip
Logging out does not delete your data. Everything is saved in the cloud and will be there when you log back in.
:::
